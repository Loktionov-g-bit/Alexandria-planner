Alexandria Planner

Questions for your manager without paper notes on their desk. An employee writes a sticker, the manager sorts it on the Eisenhower matrix and answers, the author sees the decision.

{{icon:i1}}I'm an employee {{icon:i2}}I'm a manager {{icon:i3}}I'm an administrator
01

What it is

Employees used to leave paper notes on their manager's desk: "please approve my leave", "a client needs a discount", "my laptop broke". Notes got lost, answers were forgotten, nobody knew what had already been decided.

  1. The employee writes a stickerBriefly: what is needed and by when. You can attach a file and mark how urgent and important it is.
  2. The manager sorts itThe sticker lands in the Inbox on the board. The manager puts it into one of four quadrants and starts working on it, asks a question, delegates it or decides right away.
  3. The author gets an answerThe decision arrives in the browser and in Telegram. The author confirms they have seen it, and the sticker moves to the archive.

All of this happens in the browser, on a computer or a phone. The program runs on your company's server: the data stays there, and the project author never sees it.

{{icon:i4}}New here? Read sections 01–04 first, then the section for your role: employee, manager or administrator.
02

The Eisenhower matrix

Every sticker is judged by two questions: is it urgent, and is it important. That gives four quadrants. Each has its own colour, and you will see that colour everywhere in the program.

1 · do now

Urgent and important

On fire and affects results. For example, a client contract that has to be signed today.

2 · schedule

Important, not urgent

Affects results, but there is time. A monthly report, training, plans.

3 · delegate

Urgent, not important

Needs to be quick, but not necessarily by the manager. Update the price list, order business cards.

4 · eliminate

Not urgent, not important

Can wait a long time or be declined. Order water, buy a coffee machine.

{{icon:i5}}When sending, the employee adds their own estimate ("Urgent", "Important"). The manager chooses the final quadrant. When the two differ, it shows up in the statistics: this is how a team learns to see priorities the same way.
03

Who is who

There are three roles. One person can hold two: the head of a small team is often the administrator too.

{{icon:i6}}

Employee

Writes stickers to the manager or colleagues, answers questions, confirms decisions. Sees only their own stickers and those shared with them.

{{icon:i7}}

Manager

Receives the team's stickers on the board, sorts them into quadrants, answers, delegates, reviews statistics and exports the Excel report.

{{icon:i8}}

Administrator

Adds people and teams, resets passwords, connects the Telegram bot and manages updates. Sees the sign-in log.

04

Signing in and security

The administrator gives you a login and a temporary password. A login usually looks like the first letter of the first name plus the last name: j.porter.

Two-factor protection (2FA)

It is mandatory for managers and administrators: on the first sign-in the program shows a QR code. Install Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or a similar app on your phone, scan the code and enter the 6 digits. From then on, every sign-in asks for a fresh code from the app.

{{icon:i9}}Forgot your password or lost the phone with the app? Ask your administrator: they will set a new password or reset 2FA under "Administration → Users". There is no e-mail recovery; it is safer that way.

For employees

how to ask and get an answer
05

Send a sticker

Click "New sticker" in the top right corner of any page. A short form opens.

  1. ToYour manager by default. You can pick a colleague or "Me (personal sticker)": only you see it, which makes it a handy reminder.
  2. Title and detailsA one-line title: what it is about. Details and deadlines go below.
  3. Your estimatePress "Urgent" and/or "Important" if that is the case. It is a hint for the manager, who chooses the final quadrant.
  4. Deadline and filesIf you need an answer by a date, set a deadline: two days before it the sticker becomes urgent on its own. Files up to 25 MB.
06

Follow the answer

"Sent" shows what is happening with your stickers. They sit in columns, one per step.

  1. Your answer is neededWhen the manager asks something, the question is pinned at the top of the page. The sticker waits for you until you answer.
  2. AnswerType your answer right here and press "Answer". The sticker goes back to the manager.
  3. Awaiting triageThe manager has not opened the sticker yet.
  4. In progressThe sticker has been sorted. Its colour shows the quadrant the manager chose.
  5. ResolvedThere is an answer. Open the sticker, read the decision and press "Confirm receipt": the sticker closes and moves to the archive. If the answer does not work for you, press "Reopen" and explain why.
{{icon:i10}}Changed your mind? While the manager has not sorted the sticker yet ("Awaiting triage" column), you can take it back with "Withdraw" on the card.

For managers

sorting the inbox and answering
07

The board

The manager's main screen. The day's summary at the top, below it the inbox and the four-quadrant matrix.

  1. BellNew events: stickers, answers, reports from assignees. The number shows how many are unread.
  2. Progress ringHow many stickers on the board are already sorted. The goal for the day: an empty Inbox.
  3. Search and new stickerSearch by title, text and number. Managers can write stickers too: to themselves, to an employee or to their own manager.
  4. SummaryHow many wait for triage, how many are urgent, what is overdue and how many of your stickers wait for your answer.
  5. InboxNew, not yet sorted stickers. Drag a sticker with the mouse (with your finger on a phone) into a quadrant, or click it to open.
  6. QuadrantsSorted stickers. You can drag them between quadrants at any time. A red date means the deadline has passed.
{{icon:i11}}There is a dark theme: "Profile → Appearance". By default the program follows your system setting.

Sort one by one

When the inbox is full, press "Sort one by one". Stickers open one at a time: keys 1–4 file the sticker into a quadrant, Enter opens the card, → skips, ← goes back, Esc closes. The quadrant the author suggested is highlighted.

08

The sticker card

Click any sticker to open its card. On the left the text and the conversation, on the right everything you can do with the sticker.

  1. Comments and filesThe conversation about the sticker. The author sees a comment right away.
  2. ProgressWhere the sticker is now: new, sorted, in progress, resolved, closed.
  3. QuadrantClick a quadrant to put the sticker there. Below you can see how the author estimated it.
  4. DeadlineSet it, change it or remove it.
  5. ActionsThe buttons depend on the sticker's state and your role. More in the next section.

At the bottom right, the "Change log": who changed what in the sticker, and when.

09

Decisions and other actions

To answer the author, press "Resolve", choose an outcome and write a few words. The answer goes to the author immediately.

Outcomes

ApprovedYes, go ahead.
DeclinedNo, with an explanation.
DoneTaken care of: access granted, document signed.
For informationNoted, no action needed.

All actions

ButtonWhat happens
StartYou are working on the sticker. The author sees the status "In progress".
AskAsk the author a question. The sticker waits for the answer, and the question is pinned in the author's "Sent".
DelegateHand it to an employee. The sticker appears on their board, they report back with "Report", and you accept the work or send it back with "Send back".
SnoozeTake it off the board until tomorrow, for a week or until a date. On that day the sticker comes back on its own.
RejectDecline without going into it, giving a reason.
ShareShow the sticker to a colleague, for example your deputy. They will see it in their "Sent".
{{icon:i12}}The program watches deadlines for you: two days before the deadline a sticker becomes urgent, overdue ones are highlighted in red, and resolved stickers the author has not confirmed close automatically after three days.
10

Archive

Closed, rejected and withdrawn stickers are filed in the archive by month. Each one carries a stamp with its outcome.

11

Statistics and Excel

How many questions come in, how fast you answer, and which employees see priorities differently from you.

  1. PeriodDay, week, month, quarter or your own dates.
  2. Download ExcelA report for the chosen period on three sheets: "Summary", "Stickers" and "By person". Handy to attach to a report for your own manager.
  3. Period totalsHow many came in, were resolved, remain and are overdue.
  4. SpeedAverage time to triage, to a decision and to the author's confirmation. Below: how often the author's estimate matched your decision.
  5. From whomFor each employee: how many they sent, how many were resolved, response time and estimate mismatches.
{{icon:i13}}Managers do not read the stickers employees send each other: the statistics show only how many there were and the average response time.

For everyone

notifications, profile, phone
12

Notifications

There are three ways to learn about a new sticker, question or decision.

  1. BellAlways works while the page is open. Click an event to open the sticker.
  2. Browser notificationsArrive even when the tab is closed. Turn them on under "Profile → Notifications", separately on each device. Requires a secure address (https).
  3. TelegramIf the administrator has connected a bot: "Profile → Telegram → Link", then "Start" in the bot. In Telegram you can answer with buttons and create stickers.

Digests

The morning digest arrives at the time you choose on working days: what awaits you today, what is urgent, deadlines and questions for you. The evening summary arrives at 18:00. Both are set up in the profile.

13

Profile

Personal settings. Everything is saved immediately, there is no "Save" button.

AppearanceLight, dark or as in the system. Interface language: one of seven.
TimeTime zone. Deadlines and digests follow it.
NotificationsThe browser on this device, and Telegram.
DigestsMorning digest (time and days) and the evening summary.
SecurityChange password (all other sessions are signed out) and two-factor protection.

Time off and deputy

Under "Time off and deputy" set the dates and a deputy. While you are away, new stickers addressed to you also go to the deputy, who can handle them. The sticker history shows it, and authors see "Away until …" when writing to you. "I'm back" cancels the time off.

Calendar

"Calendar → Turn on" gives a personal link with the deadlines of your stickers. Add it to Google Calendar ("Other calendars → From URL"), iPhone ("Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Add Subscribed Calendar") or Outlook. Only titles are shown. If the link got into the wrong hands, press "Get a new link": the old one stops working.

14

On your phone

No separate app needed: open the Planner address in your phone's browser. Everything works the same, and you can drag stickers with your finger.

{{icon:i14}}Add Planner to your home screen and it will open like an app. iPhone: Safari → "Share" → "Add to Home Screen". Android: Chrome → menu ⋮ → "Add to Home screen". On iPhone, notifications only work in Planner opened from the home screen.

For administrators

installation and setup
15

First launch

Right after installation the program asks you to create an administrator. There are no default passwords: this account is the only way in.

First the wizard asks how you will use the Planner: as a team (people write to their manager, the manager sorts) or just me, a personal diary on the Eisenhower matrix. In a diary every sticker is personal and goes straight into a quadrant: no inbox, no Sent, no statistics, no users.

  1. Team name, first and last nameThe login is built from your name, and you can edit it.
  2. PasswordAt least 10 characters, three kinds out of four: lowercase, uppercase, digits, symbols.
  3. "I am also the manager of this team"Keep it ticked if you are the one sorting stickers. If not, untick it and assign a manager later under "Teams".
  4. 2FAScan the QR code with an app on your phone and enter the code. An empty board opens after that.
16

People and teams

The "Administration" section. Employees do not sign up themselves: the administrator adds them.

  1. Add userFirst and last name, team, role and a temporary password. The login is filled in for you. Give the person their login, password and a link to this guide.
  2. EditA new password, another team or role, 2FA reset, deactivating someone who has left. A deactivated person's stickers are kept.
  3. TeamsName and manager. By default an employee's stickers go to the manager of their team.
  4. Sign-in logWho signed in, when and from where, failed attempts and lockouts. After 5 wrong passwords sign-in is locked for 15 minutes.

Mode

"Admin → Mode" shows the current mode. A diary can grow into a team: enter the team name, you become its lead and can add people; personal stickers stay yours. Going back to a diary is possible only when there are no other active users.

17

Telegram bot

Every company has its own bot: the administrator creates it in a couple of minutes, and messages go directly between your server and Telegram.

  1. Create a botIn Telegram, open @BotFather, send /newbot and choose a name, for example "Planner Company".
  2. Bot usernameIt must end in bot, for example planner_company_bot.
  3. TokenBotFather sends a token like 123456789:AAH… Paste it and press "Connect".
  4. EmployeesEveryone links their own Telegram in the profile. You can add the bot to a team chat and write stickers there with /stiker text.
18

Updates

You can give a new version to a few people first (beta) and keep everyone else on the proven one (stable).

Every team and every person has a switch, and a personal choice overrides the team's. Moving and rolling back take effect immediately, with no downtime: the person's next request goes to the chosen version. If the test version goes down, its users automatically carry on with the main one.

{{icon:i15}}How to run the test version next to the main one is described in docs/updates.md in the repository. Before every database update the program makes a backup and keeps the last 10.
19

FAQ

 

My sticker went to the wrong person. What now?

While it has not been sorted, open it in "Sent" and press "Withdraw", then create a new one with the right recipient. If it is already in progress, leave the recipient a comment on the card.

Does my manager see my personal stickers?

No. Only you see a "Me (personal sticker)". Stickers to colleagues are seen only by you and the recipient; the manager sees just how many there were in the statistics.

Why did a sticker move to "Urgent and important" by itself?

Two days before its deadline a sticker becomes urgent. The quadrant changes but the importance stays; the card marks it as "Became urgent by deadline".

Browser notifications do not arrive.

Check three things: the address starts with https, the browser allows notifications for the site, and notifications are on under "Profile → Notifications" on this device. The "Test" button sends a test notification.

I got locked out after wrong passwords.

Wait 15 minutes or ask the administrator to set a new password.

An employee has left. Should I delete them?

No need: deactivate them under "Users → Edit → Active". They cannot sign in, and the sticker history and statistics are kept.

Which languages does the program speak?

Russian, English, German, Czech, Belarusian, Kazakh and Georgian. The language is switched under "Profile → Appearance", everyone chooses their own. Notifications, the Telegram bot and the Excel report come in the chosen language.
